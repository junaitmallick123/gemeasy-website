GeM OEM assessment

What to prepare for a GeM OEM vendor assessment

Prepare evidence of manufacturing capability before desktop and video assessment.

Read checklist
  • Check the seller profile, factory address and business records for inconsistencies.
  • Organise evidence of machinery, production capacity and quality checks for the relevant product category.
  • Plan a walkthrough so the documents and the activities you show during the video assessment correspond.

BOQ

Government tender bidding

A pre-bid checklist for government tenders

Review eligibility and delivery terms before pricing a bid.

Read checklist
  • Confirm product specifications, submission deadlines and any corrigenda or amendments.
  • Check turnover, past performance, authorisation, certificates and bid security where the tender asks for them.
  • Check the required technical and financial formats, including the BOQ template if supplied.

BIS / NABL

Testing and certification

When to look for BIS, ISO or NABL-accredited testing

Start with the exact product standard, tender clause or buyer requirement.

Read checklist
  • BIS certification can apply to defined products and Indian Standards. Check the relevant scheme and product manual.
  • For a test report, check whether the lab’s NABL accreditation scope covers the requested method or parameter.
  • If a buyer asks for an ISO management system certificate, check the standard, scope and site it needs to cover.

Performance marketing

How manufacturers can qualify B2B enquiries

Decide what counts as a useful enquiry before starting a paid campaign.

Read checklist
  • Choose a product line, serviceable region and target buyer profile.
  • Ask for product application, approximate quantity, location and a way to contact the buyer.
  • Track which enquiries your sales team can act on and use that feedback to adjust the campaign.
